Understand your kidney report

Enter individual values to get a plain-English explanation of the number you entered — including its broad range and why it matters in IgAN. This is not a diagnostic calculator.

Creatinine

A blood waste product removed by the kidneys. It is interpreted alongside other information.

eGFR

An estimate of kidney filtration. Trends over time are often more informative than one isolated result.

UPCR / UACR

Urine measurements that quantify protein or albumin loss.

Important: A single laboratory value rarely tells the whole story. Discuss your results with your healthcare professional.
