IgAN questions

Short answers to common questions. Individual situations can be very different.

Is IgAN curable?

IgAN is a chronic kidney disease, but its course varies widely. Treatment aims to reduce kidney damage and the risk of progression.

Why is protein in urine important?

Persistent proteinuria is an important marker used in IgAN risk assessment and treatment monitoring.

Does a biopsy predict my future?

A biopsy provides important information, but no single biopsy feature should be treated as a complete prediction of an individual's future.

Why do doctors monitor BP?

Blood pressure and kidney health are closely connected, so controlling BP is an important part of kidney care.

Can I interpret my results myself?

You can learn what the numbers mean, but treatment decisions require your full medical context and a qualified clinician.

How often should I test?

There is no single schedule for everyone. Your nephrologist should set the follow-up interval based on your kidney function, proteinuria and treatment.
