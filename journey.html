The IgAN journey

A simple map from the first abnormal result through diagnosis, risk assessment, treatment and monitoring.

01 · Notice

Blood or protein in the urine, swelling, high blood pressure or an unexpected kidney-test result may lead to further evaluation.

02 · Test

Blood tests, urine tests, blood pressure measurements and sometimes imaging help assess kidney health.

03 · Biopsy

A kidney biopsy can confirm IgAN and describe microscopic features using systems such as Oxford MEST-C.

04 · Assess risk

Kidney function, proteinuria, blood pressure, biopsy information and disease course are considered together.

05 · Treat & monitor

The plan focuses on kidney protection and, for appropriate patients, therapies aimed at reducing progression risk.

What should I focus on?

📊 Kidney function

Ask about eGFR and whether the trend is stable, improving or declining.

💧 Proteinuria

Ask what your urine protein measurement means and what target your clinician is using.

❤️ Blood pressure

Ask what target is appropriate for you and how it should be monitored.

🔬 Biopsy

Ask which biopsy findings matter for your individual situation.

💊 Treatment

Ask what each medicine is intended to accomplish and what monitoring is needed.

📅 Follow-up

Ask when blood and urine tests should be repeated and what changes warrant earlier contact.
